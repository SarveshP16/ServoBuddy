Your car, sorted. 🚗✨

Meet ServoBuddy, a car manager for Android that keeps everything about your car in one place:

⛽ Live NSW FuelCheck prices: see the cheapest servo nearby on a map, with your shopper-docket discounts applied
🔧 Service reminders that learn how much you actually drive
📅 Rego, insurance, green slip and pink slip reminders, with nothing extra to enter
💸 Every expense tracked: fuel, services, tolls, parking, rego, plus your real L/100 km
🎨 Seven themes, including dark mode and Red & Black

No account. No sign-up. No cloud. Your data stays on your phone. 🔒
